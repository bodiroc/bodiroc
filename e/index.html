Her story

Practicing what she teaches since 1981.

When Shelly started, anyone in Hollywood with a great body could call themselves a trainer. She chose education instead: a two-year program at UCLA, then certification by the American College of Sports Medicine.

More than four decades later, she still strength trains and does cardio three to six times a week.
